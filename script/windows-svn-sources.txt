Wu Plus packages the Slik Subversion 1.14.5 Windows client and its libraries.
Wu Plus adds a process-level UTF-8 manifest to its private copy of svn.exe.

https://sliksvn.com/download/

Apache Subversion 1.14.5 source and NOTICE:
https://archive.apache.org/dist/subversion/subversion-1.14.5.tar.bz2
